2.4 Crash Reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

To help us identify and fix errors within the app, we use Firebase Crashlytics, a service provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, in both the Android and iOS apps. If the app crashes or encounters a critical error, a crash report is transmitted to Google. This report contains information about the device (model, operating system, and version), app version, timestamp, and technical error trace. App contents (e.g., appointments, offers, messages) and your user ID are not transmitted. Data transmission to third countries (USA) cannot be ruled out; Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing a stable and error-free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Crash reports are deleted after 90 days.

For more information, please visit: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
